# Voice and tone

**One line:** Talk like a payments operator who's done a hundred installs: short, direct, specific, a little dry. No corporate filler.

## Principles

1. **Say the job, not the tech.** "Builds the menu", not "leverages AI to digitize unstructured menu data."
2. **Numbers over adjectives.** "Minutes, not days." "$49." "13/13 prices." Not "lightning-fast", "affordable", "highly accurate."
3. **Short sentences.** Most under 15 words. One idea each. Fragments are fine.
4. **Use the industry's words.** Board, install, go-live, first transaction, residuals, agent, ISO, back office, modifier group. Don't explain them to ISOs.
5. **Honest about limits.** Beta formats, review step, three POS today. Trust is the product in this industry; agents have been burned by overpromising vendors.
6. **You, not we.** The reader is the hero. "You snap the menu," not "Our platform ingests."

## Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| ISO landing page, LinkedIn | Peer-to-peer, numbers-forward | "Your install team shouldn't be typing menus." |
| Agent social, Facebook groups | Casual, practical, shop talk | "Closed a taqueria today. Menu was built before I got back to the truck." |
| Restaurant owner pages, Reels | Plain, reassuring, zero jargon | "Take a photo of your menu. Download a file your new POS will import." |
| Guides/blog | Clear, step-by-step, useful even if they never buy | "Clover's import file has four sheets. Here's what goes on each." |
| Errors, support | Calm, specific, tells them what to do | "That's a HEIC photo. Change your camera to 'Most Compatible' or screenshot it." |

## Word list

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| menu build, build the menu | digitize, ingest, onboard data |
| import file | payload, artifact, data export |
| minutes | instantly, seamlessly, in a flash |
| fix what's flagged | AI-assisted human-in-the-loop validation |
| board, install, go-live | activation journey |
| Square, Clover, Toast (plain text) | partner, certified, official, integrated |
| beta | verified, guaranteed |
| agents, ISOs, resellers | channel partners, stakeholders |
| restaurant, merchant, owner | customer success, end user |
| AI (sparingly, as a fact) | AI-powered, revolutionary, game-changing, magic, unleash, supercharge, cutting-edge, robust, leverage, empower |

## Mechanics

- Sentence case for headlines and buttons. ("Build a menu", not "Build A Menu".)
- Prices: "$49", "$149/mo", "about $15 a menu". No ".00".
- Numbers as numerals. "13/13", "4–20 hours".
- Oxford comma: no, except where needed for clarity (matches site copy: "Square, Clover or Toast").
- No exclamation points in product copy. One allowed per social post, max.
- No em-dash chains. Use a period.
- Emoji: none on the site or LinkedIn body copy. Light use on Instagram/TikTok captions.

## Do / don't

**Do**
- Open with the reader's problem in their words.
- Put a number in the first two lines.
- Show the actual product (screenshots, screen recordings of a real build of a sample menu).
- Say "beta" and "review before you download" without apology.
- End with one clear action.

**Don't**
- Lead with "AI".
- Name or imply a relationship with any POS company.
- Use fake testimonials, fake logos, fake counts ("trusted by 500 ISOs").
- Mention any employer, past or present, or any real merchant.
- Promise POS systems we don't support yet with dates.
- Market DoorDash/Uber Eats/Grubhub link import.
