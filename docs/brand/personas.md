# ICP personas

Composite personas from how restaurant boarding works in merchant services. Not real people. Validate in the first 20 customer conversations and update.

Order = priority.

---

## 1. Dana, ISO owner / VP of operations

**Who:** Runs or co-owns an ISO with 10–60 agents (W-2 and 1099). Sells processing plus Clover, Square, sometimes Toast or SkyTab. Boards 20–150 restaurants a month. Former agent; reads residual reports more than email.

**What she's measured on:** net new merchants, approval-to-first-transaction time, attrition in the first 90 days, residuals.

**Where the menu build hurts her:**
- Her onboarding/install team spends hours per restaurant on menu entry, which is unbillable.
- Go-lives slip because "we're waiting on the menu." A merchant who signed but hasn't run a card is a cancellation risk.
- Agents promise "we'll set everything up" to close, then hand the menu to ops.
- POS-switch deals (the best deals: they come with volume) stall on re-entering a 150-item menu.

**What she wants to hear:** hours back per install, merchants processing sooner, a predictable cost per board, something her team can run without training.

**Objections:** "Will my team actually use it?" "What does it cost at our volume?" "Does it work for the POS we sell?" "Is my merchants' data safe?"

**Where she is:** LinkedIn, ETA (Electronic Transactions Association) events, Midwest/Southeast/Western States acquirer associations, ISO-focused Facebook groups, industry newsletters and podcasts, direct referrals from processors' ISO channel managers.

**Offer that fits:** Pro ($149/mo for 10 builds, ~$15/menu) when it ships; until then, per-menu with a promo code for her team, plus a founder call.

**Message:** *Your install team shouldn't be typing menus. Cut menu builds from hours to minutes and get merchants processing sooner.*

## 2. Marcus, independent sales agent

**Who:** 1099 agent, 3–15 years in. Sells processing and POS to restaurants in his metro. Closes 2–8 restaurants a month. Works out of his truck and his phone. Often does the install himself to protect the deal.

**Where the menu build hurts him:**
- Nights and weekends typing menus into a back office so the install can happen.
- Or he waits on the ISO's onboarding queue and the merchant gets cold feet.
- On a POS switch, the merchant's #1 fear is "I have to rebuild my whole menu."

**What he wants:** close faster, install faster, look like a pro in front of the owner. "I'll have your menu built before I leave."

**Objections:** "$49 is coming out of my pocket." (Answer: it's less than an hour of your time, and less than a lost deal.) "What if it gets stuff wrong?" (You review it before you pay; flagged items are marked.)

**Where he is:** Facebook groups for merchant services agents, Reddit (r/merchantservices, r/CreditCardProcessing-type communities), YouTube, LinkedIn (less active, still reads), text threads with other agents.

**Offer:** $49 per menu, pay at download. Use it in the meeting: "Hand me your menu, give me two minutes."

**Message:** *Snap the menu at the table. Download the Clover file before you leave the parking lot.*

## 3. Priya, POS reseller / installer (VAR)

**Who:** Runs a 2–10 person POS dealer or install shop. Sells and installs hardware, programs menus, does training. Often authorized by one or more POS brands; also takes conversion jobs. Charges for setup, sometimes per hour.

**Where the menu build hurts her:**
- Menu programming is the biggest labor line in an install and the hardest to quote.
- Every new POS has a different import sheet and quirks (Square can't create modifier sets from the sheet; Toast bulk import needs existing group GUIDs; Clover links by name).
- Conversions between POS brands mean re-keying everything.

**What she wants:** a clean first draft in the right format so her techs only fix and finish. Predictable cost so she can quote a flat install price and keep the margin.

**Objections:** "We already have templates." "How do you handle modifier min/max, sizes, nested options?" "Can I trust the output on a 300-item menu?" She will test it hard.

**Where she is:** LinkedIn, POS dealer forums and Facebook groups, YouTube how-tos, distributor events.

**Offer:** Pro (volume), later an agency tier. Today: $49/menu with a reseller promo code.

**Message:** *Quote the install, not the hours. A clean draft of the menu in minutes, in the import format for the POS you're installing.*

## 4. Tony, restaurant owner switching POS (secondary)

**Who:** Owns 1–3 locations (taqueria, pizza, cafe, bar). Leaving a POS because of fees, hardware, support or a contract ending. Not technical. Does everything on his phone after close.

**Where it hurts:** The menu is the scariest part of switching. 80–200 items, sizes, add-ons, combos. His new POS rep either charges for the build or hands him a spreadsheet template.

**What he wants:** not to spend his Sunday retyping. Wants it to look right the first time.

**Objections:** "Is this a scam?" "Will it work with my new POS?" "I'm not good with computers."

**Where he is:** Google search ("how to import menu into Clover", "move menu from Toast to Square"), Facebook/Instagram, TikTok/Reels, YouTube how-tos, restaurant owner Facebook groups.

**Offer:** $49 one time. Build and review free, pay at download.

**Message:** *Switching POS? Don't retype your menu. Take a photo. Download a file your new POS will import.*

## Anti-personas (don't chase)

- Enterprise chains with a menu-management team (need integrations, SSO, procurement).
- Square-only single-location owners who are fine waiting 24 hours for Square's free import. We'll take them, but don't spend ad money on them.
- Anyone wanting us to pull menus from DoorDash/Uber Eats/Grubhub links.
