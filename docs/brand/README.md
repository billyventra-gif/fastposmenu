## Positioning statement

**For** ISOs, independent sales agents and POS resellers who board restaurants every month,
**who** lose hours (sometimes days) building each merchant's menu before the first transaction,
**Fast POS Menu** is a menu builder
**that** turns the menu the restaurant already has (phone photos, PDF, spreadsheet or an old-POS export) into an import file for Square, Clover or Toast in minutes.
**Unlike** the free importers built into Square, Lightspeed and Aldelo, which only feed their own POS, or Toast's ~$150/hr menu builds and the hand-programmed builds most onboarding teams still do,
**we** work across POS systems, take any input, and let the agent review and fix the menu on a phone before anything is imported.

**Short version (internal):** cross-POS menu builds in minutes, built for the people who board restaurants.

### Category

We don't call it "AI menu digitization" or "OCR." Merchant-services people buy outcomes. Category language, in order of preference:

1. **Menu builds** ("menu build in minutes", "skip the menu build")
2. **POS menu import / menu migration** (SEO and restaurant-owner side)
3. **Boarding / onboarding tool** (ISO side, when talking about where it fits in their process)

### Who it's for, ranked

1. **ISOs** (owner/ops lead): the economic buyer. Pays for Pro when it ships.
2. **Independent sales agents:** the daily user. Buys per menu today, pushes the ISO to go monthly.
3. **POS resellers / installers / VARs:** high volume, technical, the most price-aware.
4. **Restaurant owners switching POS:** secondary. Self-serve, one menu, pays $49 once.

## Tagline options

Ranked. **Recommendation: #1 for ISO surfaces, keep "Any menu. Any POS. In minutes." as the master brand line.**

1. **Board the restaurant. Skip the menu build.** — names the job and the pain in ISO language.
2. **Menus in minutes. Merchants live sooner.** — ties to first-transaction speed.
3. **From menu photo to POS import.** — literal; best for paid search and video captions.
4. **The menu build, handled.** — short; good for LinkedIn banners.
5. **Stop retyping menus.** — the owner/agent gut-punch; good hook for short video.
6. **Any menu. Any POS. In minutes.** — current master line. Broad, memorable, already on the site.

Rejected: anything with "revolutionize", "AI-powered" as the lead, "seamless", "magic". See [voice.md](voice.md).

## Naming and legal rules

- Product name: **Fast POS Menu**. Always three words, "POS" in caps. Never "FastPOSMenu", "FPM" (in public copy), or "Fast POS".
- Parent: **Code 85 Labs** (sign-off: "A Code 85 Labs product."). Domain mentions: code85.ai.
- **POS names are factual only.** Write "Square", "Clover", "Toast" as plain text. No vendor logos, colors-as-logos, or lockups. Never "partner", "certified", "official", "approved", "integrated with" or "verified". Approved phrasing: "builds an import file formatted for Clover", "for Square, Clover and Toast". Add the trademark line on POS pages: *Square, Clover and Toast are trademarks of their respective owners. Fast POS Menu is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.*
- **Import formats are beta.** Until a generated file imports clean into a real account for each POS, say "beta" where formats are discussed. Never "guaranteed to import" or "100% accurate."
- **No marketplace link import.** We don't import from DoorDash, Uber Eats or Grubhub links and don't market it. Copy says: "Screenshot it and upload the screenshot."
- **No employer references.** Fast POS Menu and Code 85 Labs are independent. No mention of any other payments company Billy has worked with, their merchants, data or relationships. Sample menus are made up (e.g. "Riverbend", "Casa Lupita Taqueria").
