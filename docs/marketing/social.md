## Strategy in five lines

1. **LinkedIn is the ISO channel.** Post from **Billy's personal profile** (people follow operators, not logos), mirror to a Code 85 Labs / Fast POS Menu company page. Weekdays, 7–8am PT.
2. **Facebook groups are the agent channel.** Merchant-services agent groups and POS reseller groups. Value first, product second, and only where group rules allow promotion. 2x a week max.
3. **Short video is the owner channel and the demo engine.** Instagram Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts: "menu photo → POS import in 60 seconds" before/afters. 3x a week. The same clips get reused on LinkedIn.
4. **One idea per week**, told several ways: W1 launch + the problem, W2 how it works + objections, W3 POS switches, W4 numbers + first customer stories (only real, permissioned ones).
5. **Every post has one CTA and one link**, UTM-tagged (see `paid.md` → tracking). Links in LinkedIn posts go in the first comment.

- Formats are beta. Don't say "imports perfectly." Say "import file", "review before you download".
- No fake testimonials, counts or logos.

> Moving a restaurant from Toast to Square? Here's the part nobody tells the owner: Toast often does sizes as a modifier group. Square wants them as variations.
>
> Copy them over as modifiers and the reporting is wrong for the life of the account.
>
> Wrote up the whole switch, start to finish. Guide in the comments.

**Wed Dec 2 · Carousel: one menu, three POS** (6 slides)
1. "Same latte. Three POS systems. Three different ways to build it."

4. Toast: "Sizes are a group priced by modifier. The bulk tool needs your menu group IDs."
